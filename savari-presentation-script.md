# Savari — Presentation Script

**Length:** about 6–7 minutes spoken (about 900 words)
**Format:** 10 slides. Each slide has a **[SLIDE]** cue, the words to say, and short stage notes in *italics*.
**Placeholders:** Fill in anything in `[brackets]` with real numbers before you present.

>

> That's Savari.

## 3. The problem
**[SLIDE: A cluttered phone screen full of separate app icons]**

> Our lives are spread across too many apps.
>
> - **Riders** spend more time booking than moving.
> - **Drivers** give a large share of every fare to the platform.
> - **Small businesses** like shops, restaurants, and service providers pay to be on many platforms just to be found.
> - **Payments** get split across wallets, cards, and cash.
>
> It's a lot of switching, a lot of stress, and a lot of money lost along the way.

## 5. Meet Savi (the centerpiece)

*If you can, show a live demo or a 20-second screen recording here. It is the strongest part of the pitch.*

## 6. Drivers: zero commission
**[SLIDE: Big "0%" in large type]**

>
> **Savari takes zero commission.** Drivers keep everything they earn on every ride.
>
> Partners pay a simple monthly subscription instead. That's a predictable cost, not a cut of each fare that grows as they work more.
>

## 7. Safety and trust
**[SLIDE: Shield icon with four short points]**

> A platform that moves people has to earn their trust. Here's how we do it:
>
> - **Verified drivers:** government ID, driving license, and background checks before anyone is approved.
> - **Choice for women:** women can choose female drivers, and female drivers can choose to accept only female passengers.
> - **SOS in the app:** one tap sends an alert to Savari support or to your trusted contacts.
> - **Live tracking and trip sharing**, so someone always knows where you are.
>
> Payments go through encrypted gateways, and we don't store sensitive card data on our servers.

## Before you present, check these
- **Add real metrics.** The website doesn't publish user, driver, or city numbers. Even one or two real numbers will make the pitch much stronger.
- **Partner subscription price.** Have the figure ready, because drivers and investors will ask.
- **Availability.** Know which cities are live. Service only starts in an area once enough people there sign up.
- **Avoid unverifiable superlatives** like "world's first" in front of investors. Show it with a demo instead.
